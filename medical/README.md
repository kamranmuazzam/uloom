# Medical Sciences

The medical knowledge repository is organized into two primary divisions:

## 1. [Basic Sciences](basic_sciences/)
Foundational pre-clinical and para-clinical sciences:
- **[anatomy/](basic_sciences/anatomy/)**: Gross anatomy, osteology, histology (bone blood supply, pancreas anatomy).
- **[physiology/](basic_sciences/physiology/)**: Organ physiology, normal cardiovascular mechanisms, biophysics.
- **[biochemistry/](basic_sciences/biochemistry/)**: Metabolism, clinical biochemistry, nutrition.
- **[pathology/](basic_sciences/pathology/)**: General, systemic, and clinical pathology.
- **[pharmacology/](basic_sciences/pharmacology/)**: Drug actions, therapeutics, and toxicology.
- **[microbiology/](basic_sciences/microbiology/)**: Bacteriology, virology, parasitology, immunology.
- **[forensic_medicine/](basic_sciences/forensic_medicine/)**: Medical jurisprudence, thanatology, toxicology.
- **[community_medicine/](basic_sciences/community_medicine/)**: Epidemiology, biostatistics, public health.

## 2. [Advanced Sciences](advanced_sciences/)
Clinical disciplines, clinical cases, and exam question banks:
- **[clinical/](advanced_sciences/clinical/)**: Bedside examination cases (Medicine, Surgery, ObGyn, Eye).
- **[medicine/](advanced_sciences/medicine/)**: Final Prof Internal Medicine past papers, MCQs, viva notes, and daily exam reviews.
- **[documents/](advanced_sciences/documents/)**: Integrated curriculum texts (General Surgery, ObGyn, Medicine, Question Bank).
- **[syrgnsaq/](advanced_sciences/syrgnsaq/)**: Surgery & ObGyn SAQ papers (2022M–2025N).
- **[medical_science/](advanced_sciences/medical_science/)**: Cardiology reference material.
- **[old/](advanced_sciences/old/)**: Archived TeX surgical monographs.
