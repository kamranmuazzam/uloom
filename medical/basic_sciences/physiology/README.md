# Physiology

Human physiology principles, organ systems, and biophysics:
- **General Physiology & Cell**: Homeostasis, membrane transport, resting membrane potential, action potential.
- **Cardiovascular System**: Cardiac cycle, ECG, hemodynamics, blood pressure regulation.
  - `cardiovascular_normal.typ`: Normal cardiac physiology notes.
- **Respiratory System**: Mechanics of breathing, gas exchange, regulation of respiration.
- **Renal System**: Glomerular filtration, tubular reabsorption, countercurrent mechanism, acid-base balance.
- **Gastrointestinal System**: Secretions, motility, digestion, and absorption.
- **Endocrinology & Reproduction**: Hormonal axes, feedback loops, reproductive physiology.
- **Nervous System & Special Senses**: Sensory/motor pathways, reflexes, autonomic regulation.
- **Biophysics**:
  - `biophysics_waves.typ`: Biophysical principles of wave mechanics.
