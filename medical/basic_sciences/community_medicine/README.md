# Community Medicine

Public health, disease prevention, and healthcare delivery:
- **Epidemiology**: Principles, epidemiological study designs (cohort, case-control, RCT), screening tests.
- **Biostatistics**: Measures of central tendency, dispersion, probability, tests of significance.
- **Maternal & Child Health (MCH)**: Antenatal care, infant mortality, immunization schedules (EPI).
- **Infectious Disease Control**: Epidemic investigation, outbreak control, vector-borne diseases.
- **Environmental Health**: Water purification, sanitation, waste management, occupational hazards.
- **Health Systems & Policy**: Primary health care, national health programs, international health agencies (WHO, UNICEF).
