# Forensic Medicine & Toxicology

Application of medical knowledge to legal matters and investigation of toxins:
- **Forensic Pathology**: Thanatology, signs of death, post-mortem changes.
- **Thanatology & Mechanical Injuries**: Wounds (abrasions, contusions, lacerations, incised, stab, firearm), injury mechanics.
- **Asphyxial Deaths**: Hanging, strangulation, suffocation, drowning.
- **Medical Jurisprudence & Ethics**: Medical council acts, informed consent, medical negligence, professional misconduct.
- **Toxicology**: Corrosives, metallic poisons, neurotoxins, agricultural poisons, drug overdoses.
