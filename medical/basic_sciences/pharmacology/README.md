# Pharmacology

Study of drug action, pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, and therapeutics:
- **General Pharmacology**: Absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, receptor theory.
- **Autonomic Nervous System**: Cholinergic and adrenergic agonists and antagonists.
- **Cardiovascular Pharmacology**: Antihypertensives, antianginals, antiarrhythmics, heart failure drugs.
- **Central Nervous System**: Sedatives, hypnotics, antiepileptics, analgesics, antidepressants.
- **Antimicrobial Agents**: Antibacterials, antivirals, antifungals, antiparasitics.
- **Chemotherapy & Immunopharmacology**: Antineoplastic agents, immunosuppressants.
- **Toxicology**: Drug poisoning, antidotes, adverse drug reactions.
