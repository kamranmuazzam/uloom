# Anatomy

Foundational human anatomy curriculum notes and papers:
- **Gross Anatomy**: Superior extremity, inferior extremity, thorax, abdomen, head & neck, neuroanatomy.
- **Histology**: General and systemic microscopic tissue architecture.
- **Embryology**: General and systemic development, congenital anomalies.
- **Osteology**: Skeletal framework and bony landmarks.

### Topics & Articles
- `bone/`: Blood supply of bone, histological structure, and osteology.
- `pancreas/`: Structural anatomy, ductal system, and relations.
