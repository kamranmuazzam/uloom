# Advanced Sciences (Clinical Sciences)

The clinical sciences are structured into the three core clinical divisions:

## 1. [Medicine](medicine/)
- **written_question_papers/**: Paper 1 and Paper 2 MCQs, SAQs, SBAs, and OMR templates (2020N–2025N).
- **viva/**: Oral examination notes across all internal medicine systems (CVS, Respiratory, Hepatology, Haematology, Nephrology, Neurology, Rheumatology, Endocrinology) and Paediatrics (instruments, x-rays, pictograms).
- **notes/**: 25-chapter comprehensive clinical medicine monograph covering Paper 1 and Paper 2 topics.
- **clinical/**: Bedside clinical examination presentations (Stroke, Congestive Cardiac Failure, MMSE).
- **questionbank/**: Structured question banks and solved past exam papers.
- **curriculum.typ**: National MBBS curriculum breakdown for Medicine.
- **ongoing exam details/**: Daily ward exams, case reviews, and reports.

## 2. [Surgery](surgery/)
- **general principles/**: Principles of surgery (shock, trauma, burns, hemorrhage, fluids, surgical infections, oncology, hernia, anesthesia, operative surgery).
- **clinical/**: Surgical clinical long cases (chronic cholecystitis), short cases, extra cards (breast carcinoma, etc.), and throat/tonsil examination.
- **ophthalmology/**: Eye cases, examination signs, and unanswered clinical question banks.
- **syrgnsaq/**: Surgery SAQ past papers (Paper 1 & Paper 2 from 2022M–2025N).
- **questionbank/**: Surgery question bank (Systemic Surgery, Neurosurgery, Orthopaedics, Cardiothoracic, Paediatric Surgery, Alimentary, Hepatobiliary, Urinary).
- **mcq/**: Surgery multiple-choice question collections across general principles.
- **ent/**: Ear, Nose, and Throat notes and clinical cards.
- **curriculum/**: Surgical syllabus plan, summary, and requirements.
- **old_archive/**: Archived TeX surgical monographs, fracture papers, and question pools.

## 3. [Obstetrics & Gynaecology](obs_and_gyne/)
- **obs/**: Obstetrics curriculum and pathology (antenatal care, labor, APH, PPH, GDM, pre-eclampsia/HTN, caesarean section, puerperium).
- **gyne/**: Gynaecology curriculum and pathology (contraception, infertility, uterine/cervical pathology, ovarian tumours, prolapse, menopause, gynaecological oncology).
- **clinical/**: Clinical obstetrical and gynaecological topics, history taking, and examination (`in.typ`).
- **syrgnsaq/**: ObGyn SAQ papers (Obs Paper 1 & Gynae Paper 2 from 2022M–2025N).
- **questionbank/**: Structured question banks for Obstetrics and Gynaecology.
- **curriculum.typ**: National MBBS curriculum breakdown for Obstetrics & Gynaecology.
